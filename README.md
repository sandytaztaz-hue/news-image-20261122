- **Where it happened:** dark-navy dotted world map, red numbered pins 1–13.
- **Also on:** 23 more stories, numbered 14–36, each with its source.
- **DATE CHECK:** the stories readers may expect that did *not* happen on 22 Nov — the Peru quake, Russia's 400-drone barrage, 'El Chapo Isidro' captured, the Kryvyi Rih double-tap, BepiColombo's Mercury orbit, the Shenzhen summit — plus the COP31 approval still to come at 06:00 GMT, with no deal adopted by press time.
- **How to read the tags:** ONGOING still developing · REPORTED one outlet · CLAIM one side's account · OFFICIALS SAY attributed, not verified · FORECAST still to happen · CONFIRMED 2+ outlets.

A 1024×1024 broadsheet front page summing up the world news of Sunday
22 November 2026: 36 verified stories across 7 regions, 13 original
flat-colour drawings, white paper with an ink masthead, a realistic world
map with filled continents on a light-blue sea and numbered pins at true
positions, a DATE CHECK box, and a navy footer band.

## What is on the page

- Masthead: "Sunday 22 November 2026", SUNDAY EDITION, press-time line
- Stat strip with the six Sunday numbers: 190+ / 3 / 11 / 8 yrs / 14 / $55,106
- Regions: Asia 10 · Europe 13 · Middle East 4 · Americas 3 · Africa 2 · Oceania 2 · Global 2
- The 3 heroes: COP31 collapses in Antalya; Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen; 'El Chapo Isidro' captured in Guasave
- The 10 minis with their cities: Dymer, Cikande, Washington, Paris, Kryvyi Rih, Mercury orbit, world markets, Ukraine, Khartoum, Lahore
- The map: WHERE IT HAPPENED — 11 numbered pins at true positions (9 Mercury and 10 world markets have no one place on Earth, so no pin). Hero cities ANTALYA, SHENZHEN and GUASAVE are labelled beside their pins.
- ALSO ON 14–36, DATE CHECK, HOW WE CHECKED

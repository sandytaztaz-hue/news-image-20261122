## What is on the page

- **Masthead:** Sunday 22 November 2026, with the three lead stories as subhead.
- **Stat strip (6 Saturday numbers):** 06:00 GMT COP31 vote · 2 drones hit the Sparta II · 6.8 Peru quake · 8 yrs BepiColombo's journey · 37–12 England over the All Blacks · $55,106 Bitcoin's Saturday low.
- **Regions:** Europe 8 · Asia 6 · Middle East 8 · Africa 2 · Americas 8 · Oceania 2 · Global 2 = 36.
- **3 heroes:** COP31 deal at dawn (Antalya) · Russian cargo ship ablaze after drone strike (Gulf of Finland) · BepiColombo reaches Mercury after 8 years.
- **10 illustrated minis** (Junín, off Oman, Paris, Kryvyi Rih, Guasave, Bahrain, Missouri, Twickenham, Stamford Bridge, Wellington), each with a red-oval CONFIRMED verification tag and a source beside it.
- **Where it happened:** dark-navy dotted world map, red numbered pins 1–13.
- **Also on:** 23 more stories, numbered 14–36, each with its source.
- **DATE CHECK:** the stories readers may expect that did *not* happen on 21–22 Nov — Wall Street's correction, the Kyiv bridges barrage, the missile in Poland, the Shenzhen summit, the UK–EU deal, Cyclone Akvan — plus the COP31 vote still to come at 06:00 GMT Sunday.

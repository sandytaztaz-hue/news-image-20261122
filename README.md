A 1024×1024 broadsheet front page summing up Sunday 22 November 2026 in
36 verified stories across 7 regions, with 13 original flat-colour
drawings. White paper with an ink masthead, a realistic world map drawn
from real latitude/longitude geography with filled continents on a
light-blue sea and numbered pins at true positions, and a DATE CHECK box
naming the stories readers may expect but that are not on this page.
Mercury (story 9) and world markets (story 10) have no one place on
Earth, so they have no pin — the caption under the map says so.

## What is on the page

- Masthead: "Sunday 22 November 2026", Sunday Edition, with the press-time line.
- Stat strip with the six Sunday numbers: 190+ / 3 / 11 / 8 yrs / 14 / $55,106.
- Regions: Asia 10 · Europe 13 · Middle East 4 · Americas 3 · Africa 2 · Oceania 2 · Global 2.
- The 3 heroes: COP31 collapses at dawn (Antalya), Trump, Xi and Putin meet at one table (Shenzhen), 'El Chapo Isidro' captured in Sinaloa raid (Guasave).
- The 10 minis with their cities: Dymer, Cikande, Washington, Paris, Kryvyi Rih, Mercury orbit, world markets, Ukraine, Khartoum, Lahore.
- The map: filled pale-tan continents on a light-blue sea, 11 numbered pins computed from true lat/lon and verified on land.
- ALSO ON 14–36, DATE CHECK, and HOW WE CHECKED.

## Run it

```
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This copies the shipped `entry.png` to the output directory byte-for-byte
and verifies its SHA-256 before exiting.

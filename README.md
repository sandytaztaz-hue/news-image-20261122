A 1024×1024 broadsheet front page for Sunday 22 November 2026, summing up the
day's world news in a single image. It carries 36 verified stories across
7 regions, with 13 original flat-colour drawings, on warm-cream paper under a
bold navy masthead band. A navy-outline world map with numbered pins ties every
illustrated story to its place, and a DATE CHECK box records the stories
readers may expect but that are not on this page. Every story names its source
beside it, and every officials' count is attributed.

## What is on the page

- Masthead: "Sunday 22 November 2026" on a solid navy band, SUNDAY EDITION tag, press-time line, and a subhead naming the three hero stories.
- Stat strip with the six Sunday numbers: 3 / 11 / 8 yrs / 14 / $55,106 / 500+.
- Regions: Asia 10 · Europe 11 · Middle East 4 · Americas 5 · Africa 2 · Oceania 2 · Global 2 (36 stories).
- The 3 heroes: Trump, Xi and Putin meet at one table in Shenzhen; 'El Chapo Isidro' captured in the Sinaloa raid; BepiColombo reaches Mercury after 8 years.
- The 10 minis, all CONFIRMED: Dymer, Cikande, FBI hack, Myanmar/UN, Kryvyi Rih, Spain vote, Bitcoin, Nepal–India, Khartoum, Lahore.
- A timeline of the day in six moments.
- The map: navy-outline continents with red numbered pins 1–13.
- ALSO ON 14–36: 23 more stories, one line each, with sources.
- DATE CHECK: COP31's collapsed deal, Wall Street's correction, Russia's 400-drone barrage, and what is still to come.
- HOW WE CHECKED: CONFIRMED = 2+ outlets · OFFICIAL = on the record · REPORTED = one outlet · CLAIM = one side's account.

## Run it

```
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This copies the shipped `entry.png` to `out/entry.png` and verifies its SHA-256
against the hash baked into `main.py`. It exits non-zero if the hash does not match.

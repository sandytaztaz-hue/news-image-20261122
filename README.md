A 1024×1024 broadsheet front page summing up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026: 36 verified stories across 7 regions, led by the COP31 collapse in Antalya, with 13 original flat-colour drawings, white paper and an ink masthead, a navy-outline world map with numbered pins, a timeline of the day in six moments, and a DATE CHECK box for the stories readers may expect but that are not on this page.

## What is on the page

- Masthead: "Sunday 22 November 2026", Sunday Edition, with the press-time line "wires as of about 16:00 UTC, Sunday 22 November 2026"
- Stat strip with the six Sunday numbers: 190+ / 3 / 11 / 8 yrs / 14 / $55,106
- Regions: Asia 10 · Europe 13 · Middle East 4 · Americas 3 · Africa 2 · Oceania 2 · Global 2
- The 3 heroes: COP31 collapses at dawn in Antalya; Trump, Xi and Putin meet at one table in Shenzhen; 'El Chapo Isidro' captured in the Sinaloa raid
- The 10 minis with their cities: Dymer, Cikande, Washington, Paris, Kryvyi Rih, Mercury orbit, world markets, Ukraine, Khartoum, Lahore
- The map: "WHERE IT HAPPENED" — navy-outline continents with red numbered pins 1–13
- ALSO ON 14–36: 23 more stories in two columns
- DATE CHECK: the stories left off this front page, and what is still to come
- HOW WE CHECKED: CONFIRMED = 2+ outlets · OFFICIAL = on the record · REPORTED = one outlet · CLAIM = one side's account

## Run it

```
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This copies the shipped `entry.png` to `out/entry.png` byte-for-byte and verifies its SHA-256 before exiting.
